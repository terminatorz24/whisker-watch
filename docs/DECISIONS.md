# Whisker Watch V2 — Confirmed Choices and Pending Tuning
Updated 2026-10-22. This is the only active decision register. Do not revive historic choices from older commits.

## CONFIRMED by the owner
- Original cat tower-defense against varied mice and rats, guarding a Fish Stash or similar objective. Gameplay and visual usability strongly inspired by Bloons TD 6, but all art and distinct expressive elements original.
- Five real playable maps plus tutorial. Main maps available immediately; additional maps may arrive in later updates.
- Two lengths: a shorter Quick Run and 100-wave Full Campaign. Exact Quick wave count and boss balance are initial recommendations; user liked the separation.
- Easy, Normal, Hard; values subject to testing.
- Questions before EVERY wave, and never during combat; no deadline or countdown in planning; player manually starts the wave.
- Placement, purchases, upgrading, selling, retargeting and abilities allowed DURING active combat as well as between waves.
- Combat kills earn cash; correct multiple-choice answers are primary income; partial refund scales with amount actually invested; economic towers must not undermine studying.
- Sound/music absent; static scenery; actual cats, enemies and effects animate. Rich readable outlined cartoon artwork with some shading/shadows.
- Full tower variety, distinct upgrade paths, targeting and abilities. ALL cat types and ALL available upgrade paths purchasable from the beginning if the player has cash, subject to crosspath/placement and other actual combat constraints.
- Multiple-choice questions only in this version. ONE canonical JSON study-deck format, externally generated by AI from the person's source PDFs/notes and validated in the browser. User can paste JSON into the game rather than using a separate document. AI must be source-faithful and self-check format.
- Downloadable JSON save embeds deck, current state and every question's correct/incorrect counts; loading restores exact mid-wave and partial-study state and shows a summary. A learner can revise or remove questions safely and export their study report.
- No mandatory achievement, medal, mastery-classification or permanent upgrade-unlock system. File-based runs and straightforward study statistics.
- Codex should implement the project and art pipeline as independently as tools and task runtime permit. Routine per-phase owner approvals are unnecessary. User approval of final quality and explicit permission for PUBLIC DEPLOYMENT are required.

## PROPOSED settings needing actual build tests
- Full question counts: 5/7/9/11/12 across five twenty-wave bands with +3 on each boss = 895 answers.
- Quick length 40, questions 2 per wave through 20 and 3 afterward, +2 on bosses every 10 = 108.
- Full deck minimum 100 entries, Quick minimum 20; 5000 maximum; repeated source facts allowed with clear audit warning.
- Difficulty: starting cash 650; lives 200/150/100; tower/upgrade prices 0.85/1.00/1.08; enemy speeds 0.90/1.00/1.15.
- Sell refund 70% of money actually invested.
- Study budgets: 250*1.03^(n-1) Full and 250*1.04^(n-1) Quick; boss multipliers 1.20 / 1.15; pop and clear pool goals 37% / 6% of that budget; correct streak thresholds 3/5/10/20 for +5/+10/+15/+20%.
- Scholar cap +10% of base study pool; Fisher cap +5% of base study pool, conditional on actual combat pops.
- Full boss identities are starting concepts; Quick has four reduced encounters. Calibrate strengths using real simulation and playtests.
- 16 baseline cat types with three proposed candidates Ricochet, Mortar and Harbor. Quality and distinctness determine whether additions join version one; record roster and counts transparently.
- Tuning priorities: study remains main income source at 80% accuracy; 60% can still learn and compete; 95% not completely trivial; 0% cannot farm an easy victory. Real outcomes are not yet known.
