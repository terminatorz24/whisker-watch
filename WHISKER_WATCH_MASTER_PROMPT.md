## Product
A complete, original, polished, single-player browser tower-defense study game inspired by the play depth and accessible presentation of Bloons TD 6. Cat towers protect a Fish Stash from mice and rats that move along predefined paths. Original characters, artwork, route geometry, naming and branding are essential; screenshots of another game are layout/mechanics references, not assets to copy.
The game must be satisfying as a tower defense, not a decorative quiz. Difficulty, layered enemies, cat targeting, cooldowns, range, upgrades, abilities, complex waves, boss mechanics, income and constrained placement all function.

## Modes, maps, progression
Six selectable locations: tutorial Kitten Yard, and five main maps: Sunny Backyard, Farmhouse Barn, Fish Market Docks, Frosty Rooftops, Cheese Moon. All five main maps are playable immediately, each with genuinely different original paths, build zones, scenery and placement decisions. Additional maps can be added later. Any valid question deck works on any main map.
Two game lengths for all main maps:
- Quick Run: 40 waves; smaller boss encounters at waves 10, 20, 30, 40; deck minimum proposed 20 questions. Questions per wave: 2 on waves 1-20, 3 on 21-40, plus 2 before each boss = 108 scheduled attempts.
- Full Campaign: 100 waves; bosses at waves 20, 40, 60, 80, 100; deck minimum proposed 100 question entries. Questions per wave: 5 on 1-20, 7 on 21-40, 9 on 41-60, 11 on 61-80, 12 on 81-100, plus 3 before each boss = 895 scheduled attempts.
Question counts, minima and boss strength are trial values to tune against measured session length. Repeat questions across waves with spaced scheduling, especially missed questions. Within a study break, avoid presenting the same conceptual question twice if enough distinct content exists. Entries with duplicate facts are permitted and should be recognized for review purposes.
Easy, Normal, Hard use initial proposed lives 200/150/100; base starting cash 650; tower price multipliers 0.85/1.00/1.08; enemy speed multipliers 0.90/1.00/1.15. All play the chosen 40- or 100-wave length. Values remain provisional pending simulation and human feel checks.
No account, medals, leaderboard, long-term achievements, permanent upgrades, currency external to a run, or locked cat types/upgrade paths is required. The save captures the run plus learning history. Users can select any map and cat immediately.

## Exact player loop
Title -> New Run or Load Save -> choose Quick/Full, map, difficulty -> import one validated multiple-choice JSON deck or paste JSON -> inspect/edit question preview -> Study Break before wave 1 -> unlimited-time Shop/Prep -> manually Start Wave -> Combat -> Wave Results -> Study Break before the next wave -> repeat, save whenever desired.
No answering during active combat. No between-wave countdown. Players may pause at any time. During combat they may place, purchase, upgrade, sell, retarget, and use active abilities, funded by existing cash and cash actually earned from defeating enemies. Towers cannot be relocated for free; selling and buying anew incurs loss. Sell return starting proposal: 70% of base purchase plus upgrades actually paid, using exact recorded costs and difficulty multiplier; no negative-money/exploit loops. Selling frees placement space. Pause, 1x, 2x and optional 3x speed controls must work with deterministic fixed-tick simulation.

## Tower depth
Baseline 16 original cats: Dart, Yarn, Ice, Bomb, Blitz, Smash, Sniper, Night, Sky, Fire, Magic, Storm, Gadget, Scholar, Forest, Fisher. Original roles and variants may be refined to improve diversity; all should feel distinct.
Three further candidates from the owner's favorable concept review: Ricochet Cat (returning/bouncing shots), Mortar Cat (player-set artillery target), Harbor Cat (water/dock placement). These are proposed additions; include only with finished mechanics, art and balanced utility, and record exact final roster. Never count a proposed cat as implemented.
Each cat has three different upgrade paths, five tiers per path, meaningful effect descriptions, visually apparent upgrade changes, and relevant active abilities. A placed cat may invest in only two paths: one up to tier 5 and the secondary up to tier 2. All choices are unlocked from run start; price and crosspath rules are constraints, not campaign unlocks. Targeting: First, Last, Close and Strong, plus specialized targeting where meaningful. Test upgrade effects rather than only validating JSON entries. In-game art, tower portrait, price, sell price, range, path choices, selected upgrades and cooldowns must display correctly.
Scholar: small, capped additional cash when the player gets answers correct; no passive question answering. Fisher: small, capped reward tied to genuine combat pops or wave conditions, not an unchecked cash printer. Set precise cap and stacking in configuration and verify low-accuracy/zero-answer strategies cannot replace studying.

## Enemies and bosses
Original, readable evolving enemy family: Field Mouse -> House Mouse -> Garden Rat -> Alley Rat -> Sewer Rat; specialized fast, frost-resistant, explosion-resistant, Sneaky (detection required), Regrow, Fortified and Cheese-Armored rats; giant rat classes with higher health and splitting behavior.
Develop wave escalation inspired by established tower-defense pacing: early introduction and layering; midgame speed/detection/armor counterchecks; late-game grouped rushes and giant enemies. Standard enemies follow defined child-spawn chains. Award combat cash for actual defeat/layer events once only, never for fake damage or repeated child spawns.
Five Full bosses as working concepts: Matriarch Rat Queen (summons), Pied Piper (control/speed), Mecha Rat (shields/rotating resistance), Rat Emperor (targets defenses/resurrects elites), Great Rat Overlord (combined threat). Quick encounters on 10/20/30/40 are intentionally smaller and follow their own tuned schedule. Bosses are working original concepts, not reproduced proprietary art or encounters.
One data-driven fixed wave table per game length, difficulty scaling by config, and deterministic behavior. No automatic infinite mode is required.

## Study-only question file
One study-deck format: UTF-8 JSON for multiple-choice questions only. The learner supplies study notes or a source PDF to an EXTERNAL AI along with our precise formatter prompt, and receives a compliant .json file. The browser accepts that file or pasted JSON, validates it, previews and edits questions, and never attempts to parse arbitrary source PDFs itself. Show an example template and a one-click Copy Formatter Instructions control. No account, AI model, service call, or AI API key inside the game.
Canonical deck has deckFormat, deckType="multiple_choice", deckId, title, questions. Each question has stable id, question, answer, three distinct wrong options, brief source-grounded explanation where supported, and optional topic/tier. The AI must not invent unsupported facts, answers or explanations; when insufficient material is provided, omit unsupported questions and explain what input is missing. See docs/DECK_FORMAT.md and docs/AI_FORMATTER_PROMPT.md.
Allow exact or meaningful repeated source questions, with unique stable IDs and understandable duplicate warnings. Shorter actual source sets may be repeated by the external formatter only if the user explicitly requested that behavior. Export canonical deck JSON. No unsafe HTML rendering. Editing/removing deck questions between waves must preserve unchanged-ID study counts and safely warn/reset counts for materially edited questions.

## Learning and cash loop
Before each wave, draw the configured question count from the active deck. Include unseen and recently missed material, and space repeats when possible. Present question with four shuffled answer choices, large touch targets, no time limit, optional informational timer, and correct answer/explanation feedback after submission.
First submitted answer determines accuracy and reward: correct yields cash, wrong yields zero cash. Skipping, if offered, yields zero and breaks streak. Every question tracks number correct, number incorrect, last seen, optional skips and reports. Keep raw user-supplied content associated with counts in study report/export; do not impose arbitrary "mastered" certification.
Consecutive correct-answer streak persists across waves, resets on wrong/skip, and gives a proposed progressive STUDY-cash multiplier: 3 correct +5%, 5 +10%, 10 +15%, 20+ +20%. The multiplier is bounded, not compound interest. Record streak within the exact save snapshot. Display it clearly without disrupting question reading.
Study rewards dominate cash, combat pop income matters for in-wave upgrades, and a small clear-wave bonus exists. Full mode starting proposal: base wave study pool B(n)=250*1.03^(n-1), boss multiplier 1.20; Quick: B(n)=250*1.04^(n-1), boss multiplier 1.15. A correct question earns B(n)/Q(n) times its current streak multiplier, plus separately capped eligible Scholar bonus. No game reward for typing speed. Maximum combat cash when all planned pop units are cleared targets 0.37*B(n); cleared-wave bonus targets 0.06*B(n). Allocate combat income against statically computed economic pop units, pay on actual events, and prohibit farming re-spawns or boss health. No pop credit for enemy leaks. Tune these numbers using the actual engine; docs/ECONOMY_MODEL.md has the reproducible planning analysis. Avoid scaling cash by number of questions: adding more questions should improve learning, not automatically inflate the reward pool.
Reward sufficient defensive power at 80% accuracy with good strategy; 95% accuracy should be easier but not trivial; 60% should remain meaningfully challenging, not hard-locked. Test 0% accuracy as the anti-farming case. Design scenarios for Easy/Normal/Hard, both run lengths, every map and tower archetype. Prices, RBE, attack power and wave counts must be calibrated together rather than copied blindly from another game.

## Exact portable saving
A single downloadable .json SAVE file, separate in schema from study deck but using the same easy file extension. Save embeds the active canonical deck, per-question correct/incorrect counts, deck revision info, current run mode, map, difficulty, study cursor and answers already submitted, lives, cash, wave, cat locations and upgrades, targeting, ability cooldowns, enemies, child spawns, projectiles, effects, scheduled events, PRNG streams, tick count and anything else needed to replay identically. Progress is file-based; browser memory alone is not a substitute.
Buttons: Download Save, Load Save, and optionally Copy Save JSON / Paste Save JSON for users who prefer text. Explain that pasting into a rich-text document does not create a valid .json download; paste into the game or save as plain UTF-8 JSON. Show a restored-state summary before Resume. Allow saving during paused combat and after each completed simulation tick, and during a partly completed study break. No reliance on browser background tasks. No autosave cloud account. Remind users that unsaved progress will be lost if the tab closes. Revision/validation errors must never silently corrupt or overwrite progress.
Use one canonical versioned save schema and formal migrations, checksums/integrity checks, schema validation, and round-trip/replay equivalence tests, including save during boss and after partial study submission.

## Visual design and assets
Polished, original, colorful, dimensional cartoon game, with bold outlines, readable characters, coherent colors, mild shading/shadows where helpful, static map scenery, and animated combat entities/projectiles. No sound or music. Tower level changes are visually identifiable. All art, tiles, icons and animations must be real finished assets, not basic colored placeholder circles.
HUD: map dominates view; top shows lives, cash, wave/mode, saving and speed; right/collapsible panel shows cat shop with prices, current tower details/3 upgrade paths, targeting and active abilities. Placement preview follows pointer/touch, shows build legality and range, prevents building in paths/obstacles or overlapping towers. Study overlay pauses all combat. Desktop Mac browsers and iPhone Safari/touch are targets.
Visual-reference archive: 71 PDF pages with 87 map screenshots and 18 interface screenshots; 13 representative images checked into references/images, documented in references/REFERENCE_GUIDE.md. Use for inspiration only. Do not ship reference images.
Produce game-ready SVG/PNG assets and asset manifests with stable IDs, dimensions, anchors, directional states, animation frames. External image tools (Canva/Higgsfield) are optional and must not become a mandatory paid-service dependency. Codex should make original scalable vector/sprite assets itself when external AI art tools are unavailable. Separate production assets from references. Consult docs/ART_PIPELINE.md.
